## Study Information

Our research project aims to investigate how individuals perceive and evaluate creativity in soccer actions. As a participant, you will complete a brief background questionnaire and then watch a series of short video clips showing soccer actions. For each clip, you will be asked to rate the action on dimensions including creativity, technical correctness, and aesthetic appeal.

We will collect the following data: your rating responses (numerical ratings on provided scales) along with basic background information such as age, gender, soccer experience, and country of residence. Our goal is to understand how people perceive creative actions in soccer and what factors influence these judgements. No individual participant will be analyzed for any purpose other than the aforementioned research goals.

## Data Protection

The processing of your personal data within the framework of this research project is only possible with your explicit and voluntary consent.

By agreeing to participate, you expressly consent to the German Sport University Cologne collecting, storing, and using the following data for the purpose of the research project:

- **Contact data:** your name and e-mail address
- **Background information:** age, gender, nationality, the country you have mostly lived in, your experience (in years) playing, coaching, and watching soccer, your experience playing and coaching other sports, and your soccer coaching license
- **Rating data:** your ratings of the video clips

Your ratings and background information are stored under a **randomly generated participant ID** that contains no personal information. Your name and e-mail address are used solely for administering the study (for example, to answer questions about your participation). They will **never be published**, will **not be shared with third parties**, and will be deleted once the study has been completed.

**Publication as an open dataset:** We intend to publish the rating data together with the background information (age, gender, nationality, country of residence, soccer and sports experience, coaching license) as an **open dataset** in a public research repository, so that other researchers can verify and build on our results. Before publication, all directly identifying information (name, e-mail address, participant ID) is removed. Where a combination of background characteristics could make an individual identifiable, these characteristics will be coarsened (for example, age reported in age groups) or removed, so that no conclusions about your person are possible.

- Your data will be processed in **anonymised form** for research purposes
- Scientific publications and the open dataset will contain **no personally identifiable information**
- Your contact data will be stored securely with access restricted to authorized researchers
- Data use will be strictly in accordance with legal regulations (GDPR)

You may **revoke your consent at any time** with future effect and without any disadvantage. In such a case, your personal data will be deleted immediately. The lawfulness of data processing based on consent prior to its revocation remains unaffected. Please note that data which has already been published in anonymised form cannot be withdrawn, as it can no longer be attributed to you.

## Voluntary Participation and Risks

Your participation in this study is completely voluntary. You may withdraw at any time without giving a reason and without any negative consequences.

We believe there are no known risks associated with this research study; however, as with any online activity, the risk of a breach of confidentiality is always possible. To the best of our ability, your answers in this study will remain confidential.

The German Sport University Cologne has not taken out participant insurance for this project. Liability for material and personal damages against the university and its staff is excluded, except in cases of intent or gross negligence.
